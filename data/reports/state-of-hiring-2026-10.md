## How to read these numbers

- **Coverage is not the market.** These are the roles this tracker can see, not all hiring. Adding a job board or a community source raises counts with no change in the real world, and the tracker's sources have grown over time. So treat *shares* and *rankings* as the signal and the *total* as a description of coverage.
- **Software roles.** Public company boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and similar) are filtered to software-engineering titles; the community trackers contribute their own scope. "Role type" comes from the title classifier in [`scripts/patterns.py`](../../scripts/patterns.py).
- **Levels come from the posting's own title.** A role that doesn't say its level is counted under *Level not stated*, never guessed. Senior, staff and lead titles are grouped with mid level so they can never be mistaken for entry-level roles.
- **Rules change too.** The title classifier and company-name normalisation are improved over time, so a shift in a share can come from a better rule rather than the market.
- **Work arrangement is not reported**: only some sources state it, so any split would describe those sources, not hiring.
- **Large employers dominate counts.** A handful of companies publish hundreds of openings at once; one of them changing its board moves the shares.
- **Only live, apparently-open roles are counted** — links are re-checked every run and dead ones are dropped, but no checker catches every closed posting.
- Hackathons and events are not included.
