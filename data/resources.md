# Career Resources

Free, well-known tools people use alongside the [job list](README.md) to prepare for software engineering interviews and land roles at top tech companies — worldwide, not just US-based ones. Everything here is free to start with. [← back to project overview](../README.md)

This page is hand-curated and doesn't change automatically — unlike the job tables, it isn't rebuilt by the hourly pipeline. If a link goes stale or you know a resource that belongs here, open an issue or a pull request.

## Coding Practice

| Platform | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/) | The standard coding-interview question bank, company-tagged |
| [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/) | The same style of problems organized by pattern, with free video explanations |
| [HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/) | Practice problems plus skill-certification tests some employers accept directly |
| [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/) | Competitive programming contests and a huge archive of rated problems |
| [CodeChef](https://www.codechef.com/) | Competitive programming with strong reach across Asia |
| [Exercism](https://exercism.org/) | Language-by-language practice with real human mentor feedback |
| [Codewars](https://www.codewars.com/) | Bite-sized kata-style problems, good for daily practice |

## Mock Interviews

| Platform | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [Pramp](https://www.pramp.com/) | Free peer-to-peer mock interviews, paired automatically |
| [interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/) | Anonymous mock interviews with real engineers from top companies |
| [Exponent](https://www.tryexponent.com/) | Mock interview practice and courses for SWE, PM, and data roles |
| [Big Interview](https://biginterview.com/) | Structured mock interview practice with recorded playback |

## System Design & CS Fundamentals

| Resource | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) | The most-starred free guide to system design interviews |
| [ByteByteGo](https://bytebytego.com/) | System design explained through newsletters and diagrams |
| [CS50](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) | Harvard's free, famous intro to computer science course |

## Resume & Applications

| Tool | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/) | Free guide covering resumes, behavioral questions, and interview strategy |
| [Resume Worded](https://resumeworded.com/) | Free resume and LinkedIn profile scoring against ATS filters |
| [Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com/) | Resume builder with templates aimed at tech roles |
| [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com/) | Resume builder plus a job-application tracker |
| [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) | Grammar and clarity checker for resumes and cover letters |

## Open-Source & Fellowship Programs

Real commits on your profile, often with mentorship and sometimes a stipend:

| Program | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) | Paid summer open-source contributions with mentor orgs |
| [Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.com/) | Beginner-friendly open-source contribution drive every October |
| [MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.io/) | Paid remote software engineering fellowship, open-source and industry tracks |
| [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) | Paid open-source internships for people from underrepresented groups |
